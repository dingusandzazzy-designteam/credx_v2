FAQ

The answers your operations team will ask for first.

Does CredX replace my POS or payment processor?

No. CredX runs on the value layer underneath your existing setup. Your POS, terminals, and management software all stay in place. No new hardware at the counter. Typical integration runs 4 to 8 weeks.

Is my customer really mine?

Yes. The relationship stays in your business’s name, never a competing credit facility’s. The credit and the value-back carry your brand, so the reason they come back belongs to you.

What can I do with the data?

Opt-in and de-identified, it shows which buyers return, which segments spend the most, and when demand peaks. That turns into staffing and stock decisions you no longer guess at.

How does settlement work?

Funds settle straight to your own bank account, on your existing cadence. CredX never holds merchant money. The recovery shows up as a lower effective cost, not as a rebate you have to chase.

Who handles refunds and chargebacks?

Tier-1 stays with your team, exactly as it works today. CredX operates the value-back and credit programs end to end, with a defined escalation path so your staff never debugs our systems.

What does the 4–8 week integration involve?

A kickoff, transaction-field mapping, a sandbox test, a pilot at one or two locations, then rollout. If historical data helps, we import it — only after you opt in. Most merchants touch nothing at the counter.

What happens if a customer misses a payment?

The community lender of record holds the repayment risk — not your business. You were already paid in full and upfront, so a missed payment never touches your books or your staff’s time.
