What the data economy is, and who it pays today.

The data economy is the market for information created by everyday transactions. Every purchase records who bought, what they spent, and when they return. That record has value. Today it accrues to a competing credit facility rather than to the business that earned the sale. CredX is built to give that business access to it.

Your payment processor runs the transaction, and that part works. What happens after is where the value sits. The purchase record becomes an asset held by a competing credit facility, one with no relationship to your customer and no stake in whether they come back. CredX is structured so the business that built the relationship gets access to it.

Merchant and customer data is de-identified and shared only on explicit opt-in. Participation is a choice at every step.

Mutual Ownership Acquisition Technology, in plain terms.

Mutual Ownership Acquisition Technology, or MoaT, is the principle that all three parties to a transaction hold a stake in what it produces. The merchant earns the relationship. The customer creates the value. The community lender funds the credit. MoaT is how the upside is shared between them instead of collected by a party that did none of the three.
