The Glossary

Eleven terms. Each one is defined in a single line here, and at length on its own page. Where an industry word is used two ways, this says which way we mean.

C

Closed-Loop Credit

Credit whose value can be redeemed only inside the business or network that issued it. The industry sense of the term; it says nothing about where any one program draws that edge.

E

Embedded Credit Facility

A credit line offered inside a business's own checkout and funded by a lender, so the business extends credit without carrying the credit risk.

Embedded Lending

Lending offered at the point of sale, inside the business the customer is already buying from, rather than applied for separately at a bank.

Embedded Value Platform

A platform that puts credit, value-back and access to transaction data inside a business's own brand instead of routing them through someone else's.

I

Interchange Recovery

The share of the fee on a card sale that returns to the business instead of leaving it as interchange. On this site the range runs from 40% up to 85%, depending on qualifying volume.

M

Merchant-Linked Consumer Credit

Consumer credit tied to the business where it is used, so the value the transaction produces stays with that business rather than with a third party.

Mutual Ownership Acquisition Technology (MoaT)

The principle that all three parties to a transaction — the customer, the business and the lender — hold a stake in what it produces.

O

Open-Loop Credit

Credit whose value can be redeemed outside the business or network that issued it. The counterpart to closed-loop, and again the industry sense of the term.

S

Speed-of-Use Lending

Lending decided and delivered in the moment of purchase rather than in a separate application cycle, so the decision arrives while the customer is still at the counter.

T

Transaction Data Marketplace

A market in which businesses exchange access to transaction data, on explicit consumer opt-in and under PIPEDA.

V

Value-Back Program

A program that returns value to the customer under the business's own name rather than a third party's, so the reason to come back belongs to the business.
