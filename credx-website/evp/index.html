The Whole Proposition

An Embedded Value Platform Brings Its Own Capital

An Embedded Value Platform is financial infrastructure that runs underneath a business's existing operations, adding cost recovery, data, and lending without changing what the customer sees. CredX funds the credit your customers use, carries the risk, and returns the value to the business that earned it.

Each part of the platform does something specific for your business. They are all below.

How the relationship stays yours.

Mutual Ownership Acquisition Technology is the part of the stack that keeps value inside your brand. The credit, the data, and the value-back all resolve to your business rather than to a third party. That is what makes the relationship durable: everything the customer values carries your name, so the reason they return belongs to you.

The movement behind it →

What your data becomes.

Data as a Product turns the record of your own transactions into something you can act on. Which buyers return, which segments spend the most, and when demand peaks. It is opt-in and de-identified, and the view is of your own business, for your own decisions.

Data is de-identified and shared only on explicit opt-in. Participation is a choice at every step.

Why the saving holds.

40 to 85% Of interchange recovered, depending on qualifying volume.
$30,000 Roughly, per $1M of monthly volume, staying in the business.

Roughly $30,000 of every $1M in monthly volume stays in the business instead of leaving it as interchange. What matters for a partnership is where that comes from: it is not a discount we extend and can withdraw, it is the result of the credit running on our own facility. We are paid when the facility is used, so the saving and our revenue point the same direction.

The parts that make the rest work.

BoosT

The reason a customer comes back sooner. Tiers, milestones, and cross-business offers that make the next visit worth making, funded by the value the last one created.

A network of businesses that can run offers together. Your customers find reasons to spend beyond your four walls, and other businesses' customers find you, without new infrastructure or a larger marketing budget.

Marketing
