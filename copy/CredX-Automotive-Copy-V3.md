## What changed from V3 (review this first)

- **Stream 2 now de-risks the offer.** Added the strongest line from the case study: the dealer is paid in full and upfront, the customer carries the credit, and the risk stays with CredX. This removes the biggest objection to embedded credit.
- **320% → 321%, now sourced inline.** Corrected to the case study's exact figure and attached the "PayPal embedded credit benchmark" attribution directly in the copy. The ⚠ flag is gone — a sourced number with its attribution is safe to publish. The looser "2–4× on premium upsells" stat was deliberately left out (least sourced, tied to retail categories).
- **Added a white-label beat** to the Control section: "Your dealership's name. Our infrastructure." Compresses the case study's white-label pillar into three lines.
- **Compliance strengthened.** SOC 2 + PIPEDA (Canadian privacy law) in the trust strip, plus a PIPEDA touch in the data FAQ.
- **Hardware saving added.** "No new terminals or hardware" in How It Works (SoftPOS/QR). The specific $500–$1,500/year figure is available as a callout if you want it.
- **Integration time corrected:** 2–4 weeks → **4–8 weeks**, to match the official FAQ. Under-promise.

**Deliberately kept OUT (retail contamination):** the 21-day revolving-credit term, the hunting/hockey/fishing seasonal framing, the retail product categories, and every Sylvestre-specific dollar figure. Auto F&I credit is structured differently, so none of it belongs here.

## Build notes

- **Order flipped.** The movement leads. The $30,000 saving is the cherry, not the headline.
- **Two builds, one copy.** Ship one dark and one light version and A/B test — no need to pick a theme yet.
- **Hero has a video slot.** The copy stands on its own so the page ships before the movement film is ready (AI vs. illustration still under internal review).
- **Every primary CTA anchor-scrolls to the form** at the bottom. Anyone who wants to skip the story lands straight on it.

## 9 · FAQ

**Heading**
Questions dealers ask before signing

**Does CredX replace my DMS?**
No. CredX runs on the value layer underneath your existing DMS. PBS, CDK, and Reynolds & Reynolds are all supported. Typical integration runs 4–8 weeks.

**What about a multi-store rollout?**
One master account, individual store branding, and consolidated reporting. Each rooftop sees its own savings and value-back.

**Is my customer data really mine?**
Yes. Consent-driven, opt-in by default, de-identified for analytics, and handled under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA). Your dealership owns the value-back ledger. Visa does not.

**How soon do I see savings?**
Interchange recovery starts the month after activation, reported per store and per group.

## Self-validation — Copy 101 DZ

Ran the anti-pattern scan on the V3.1 additions before delivery. Clean, with these calls:

- **The new de-risking line is concrete and dealer-facing** — "paid in full and upfront," "risk stays with CredX, never on your books." No jargon, no hype.
- **The 321% / 76% figures carry their source inline** ("PayPal embedded credit benchmark"), which is the safe practice from the 43% lesson. The unsourced 2–4× stat was left out on purpose.
- **No retail contamination.** The 21-day term, seasonal framing, and retail categories from the case study stayed out. Every example on the page is automotive (rooftop, F&I desk, service revenue, the lot).
- **Em-dashes minimized.** The new copy uses periods and commas; the stat strip and trust line were rewritten to avoid dashes.
- **Canadian spelling + Oxford comma** held throughout (white-labelled, PIPEDA, the serial comma). Note: the EVP and FAQ source PDFs use US spelling and the banned word "rewards" — none of that was copied across.
- **Compliance claims are accurate to the official FAQ** (SOC 2, PIPEDA) and the integration time now matches it (4–8 weeks).
