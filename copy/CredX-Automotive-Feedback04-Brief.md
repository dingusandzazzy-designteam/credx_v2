## Global rules

- **Audience vocabulary swap:** `dealership / dealer → business / operator`; `DMS → POS or payment processor / management software`; `rooftops / multi-store → locations / multi-location`; `F&I desk / 6 min / before they leave the lot → checkout / ~20 seconds at checkout`; `every car you sell → every service visit, every parts purchase, every accessory install`.
- **Terminology:** "competing credit facility" / "credit facility network" (never name Visa/Mastercard). Keep "embedded value platform" / "embedded lending" (never "BNPL" / "payments platform" / "payment rails").
- **House style (standing project rule, unchanged):** expand contractions in customer-facing copy (do not / you are / we will / here is). Canadian spelling (colour, centre, favour, white-labelled, PIPEDA). Oxford comma always.
- **No negative framing** (no: loses, lost, leakage, stolen, punish). Every truth framed as a gain. "rewards → value-back". Remove "reshaping payment economics" and "philanthropic" entirely.
- **No categorical guarantees:** "designed to recover", "up to", "may improve". Specific over vague.
- **Data language:** every data mention states consent-driven, opt-in by default, de-identified for analytics, handled under PIPEDA.
- **Remove role-titled personas from the Social Proof section only (user direction 2026-06-09).** In §7 ("Dealers/operators already in the network"), drop the role-attributed people (e.g. "F&I Director, Western Canada") — already handled by the §7 → "Built for…" reframe. This is **scoped to §7**, NOT a global "no people" rule; the founder pull-quote in §2 is governed separately (see Conflicts).
